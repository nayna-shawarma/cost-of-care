07 / METHODS & SOURCES

What the data can and cannot say.

The strength of a public-data project is knowing precisely where its evidence ends.

01 / SOURCE

Hospitalisation survey

NSS 75th Round, Social Consumption: Health, 2017–18. Hospitalisation, expenditure and reimbursement files are matched on the sampled household, member and admission identifiers. This site displays grouped results only.

02 / DEFINITION

Not a cancer registry

Survey ailment code 13 combines known or physician-suspected cancers with growing painless lumps. It does not identify cancer site, stage, diagnosis confirmation, or the full burden of outpatient care.

03 / ESTIMATION

Weights and suppression

Figures use the survey’s combined multiplier: weighted medians for rupee costs and weighted shares for percentages. Sample counts are unweighted. Groups below 30 observations are suppressed.

04 / MISSINGNESS

Blank is not zero

Reimbursement is summarised only when an amount was recorded. The resulting estimate is not used as a population-wide net-cost figure. Source-of-finance codes describe the major reported source, not every source a household used.

05 / INSURANCE

Coverage is not utilisation

NSS records the broad source of health-expenditure support: government-sponsored, employer-linked, household-arranged private insurance, other scheme or no cover. It does not identify a policy’s actual payment for this admission.

06 / PM-JAY

A later administrative record

The PM-JAY section uses a 2025 Ministry of Health and Family Welfare Parliamentary annexure on cancer-related admissions and treatment amount. It is presented separately from the 2017–18 NSS survey.
